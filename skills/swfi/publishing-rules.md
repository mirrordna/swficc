# SWFI Publishing Rules

Agents may create drafts and approval requests.

Agents may not:

- publish content,
- send email,
- send WhatsApp or other outbound messages,
- schedule public release,
- modify already-published content,
- expose private/subscriber-only fields.

Before any publish:

1. Draft exists.
2. Evidence is attached.
3. Internal diagnostics are removed.
4. Stakeholder approval artifact exists.
5. Human operator executes or explicitly authorizes the release.
